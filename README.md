# Multi-Income-Tracker-App
This repository demonstrates the appearance and functionality of the MoneyStar multi-income tracker app, an app designed for people with multiple streams of income to easily log their shifts and earnings using ai-assisted image scanner. Users can upload screenshots of their shifts and the app sends it to  Google Cloud for Gemini to verify the image, and extract the relevant data. Users can also manually enter shifts and earnings. In the app's dashboard and calendar view, users can view all of their hours, gross earnings, pay days and deposits across all of their current projects in one place. 

The MoneyStar app is designed for people with multiple streams of income to easily track their earnings across different platforms, clients and projects. It allows them to see how many hours they have worked, how many tasks they have completed, how much they have earned, and expected deposits for set time periods. This app is particularly useful for independent contractors who work different projects each with their own rate and payout schedule to easily track all of their income in one place. Users can set monthly or weekly goals and see their progress in real time as they receive deposits and it works as a reverse goal calculator as well showing users how many hours they have to work for different projects in order to reach their goal. 

The app is separated into four tabs: Dashboard, Earnings Calendar, Manage Platforms and Projects and Import Shifts. In the header, there are also two buttons that are always visible: Add Earnings and Log Shift.

ADD EARNINGS BUTTON: Users can add any income they received that was not from a job such as gifts, winnings or unemployment benefits.

LOG SHIFT BUTTON: Users can manually log individual shifts for one of their current projects or select "other" to add any one time gigs.

THE DASHBOARD: 
Here users can see their week-to-date gross earnings, month-to-date gross earnings, expected deposits and their month-to-date deposits received. Week-to-date and month-to-date gross earnings are based on hours, shifts, or tasks logged so far that week, or month. Expected deposits are based on the set payout schedules that land within the week and the shifts logged within the last pay period. "Expected deposits" combines all platforms that have a payout day that lands in that week while individual payouts are separated by platform and can be viewed in the calendar. The month-to-date deposits received section shows payouts that have passed. Within this sections there are two numbers, one for confirmed deposits and one for unconfirmed deposits. This allows users to confirm that a deposit that was expected actually landed in their bank account. This feature is meant to help users easily track any discrepancies in pay.
The Dashboard is also where users can see their progress towards their weekly or monthly goal and see how many hours they would have to work on any of their current projects in order to meet their goal.
Below the reverse goal calculator is the full list of all past logged shifts and payments. Users will see a line for every shift logged with the date, the platform, the project, the duration (if applicable), the rate, the # of tasks (if applicable), if there was any extra incentive on top of the regular pay rate, the gross total and if the deposit has been settled or not. Users can edit any past shift here and confirm any pending deposits that have settled. They can also sort their shift by which ones still have pending deposits vs which ones have settled, and they can set the time period for which they want to see their past shifts. 
The Dashboard defaults to the current month though users can scroll back in time to view the specs from months prior.

THE EARNINGS CALENDAR:
Here users will see the full month calendar with all logged shifts and payout dates. Shifts are separated by platform but users have to option to see a "total combined" view which instead shows them their total hours worked across all projects in one day. Payout dates are marked with the MoneyStar logo along with the expected deposit amount. Below the calendar users will see a Monthly Breakdown of all of their shifts and earnings. They can also select a day on the calendar to see a more detailed breakdown for that specific day.

MANAGE PLATFORMS AND PROJECTS:
Here users can add new platforms/clients and within them, new projects. Platforms are color coded and have a set payout schedule. Projects have their own rate, overtime rules and time caps. When a project is over, or when the user stops all work for a project, they can mark it as "done" and then it will not show up in the reverse goal calculator or as an option when manual logging shifts or earnings. It will still show up in the "Manage Platforms and Projects sections" in a separate greyed out list to show that it is not a current project. Projects and platforms can be edited at any time.

IMPORT SHIFTS:
Here is where the true functionality of this app lies and where the app uses ai to assist the user. Users upload PNG, JPEG and WEBP files from their payroll platforms or project hubs. The app sends the file to the backend to verify the screenshot and extract data. Google Gemini 3.5 Flash Lite through Vertex AI first checks the image for any prohibited content, if the image passes, Gemini scans the image and pulls out all relevant data and prepare rows for the user to review. Gemini is instructed not to fill in missing information. It generates a table with the work date, platform and project, duration, number of tasks, rate, and earnings or bonuses. It checks for any duplicates when multiple screenshots are uploaded and verifies with the user. The user then reviews all extracted data, edits anything and fills in any gaps. In case Gemini fails to extract all of the data from the screenshot or an error occurs, users can instruct the app to rescan the image. Once the user confirms the information, the shifts get added to the users calendar and shift log. Users can also upload CSV files which get handled locally and do not get sent to Google Cloud for Gemini processing. 

Gemini is responsible for reading information from the screenshot while MoneyStar is responsible for matching entries to the users projects, checking for duplicates and saving the shift information. MoneyStar does not save any image in local storage,  but as per the privacy policy, it cannot guarantee that the image will be immediately erased from Google Cloud. Browser memory, network buffers, and Google’s systems are outside the app’s control. Users should never upload images with sensitive information such as bank account numbers or social security numbers. Google’s own data handling terms apply to images sent for processing.
